# Mercurey named-site supplement

The historical named-site seed used by the simulator contained 32 Mercurey Premier Cru climat identities. The current 2025 homologated Mercurey specification includes `Clos du Château de Montaigu` as a Premier Cru climat. The identity is therefore loaded from `named_sites_supplement.json` with direct Ministry provenance rather than rewriting the older bulk seed or attributing the new record to a source that did not contain it.

The site-claim layer has two Mercurey identity-evidence routes:

- the original `bourgogne_mercurey` evidence route for the legacy 32 climat records;
- the direct `mercurey_masa_2025` route for Ministry-backed supplemental records.

Both routes depend on the same reviewed 2025 Mercurey legal specification for claim legality.
