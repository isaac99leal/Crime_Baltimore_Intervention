# Burgundy legal promotion: Ladoix and site matrices

Reviewed 2026-09-05.

## Ladoix

Effective legal source: French Ministry of Agriculture, *Cahier des charges de l'appellation d'origine contrôlée « Ladoix »*, homologated by decree no. 2011-1559 of 15 November 2011.

Source: https://info.agriculture.gouv.fr/boagri/document_administratif-64c27bea-ac3a-4400-be93-525ce351fdc4/telechargement

The ministry publication record reports the 2011 specification as published and not abrogated or modified. A later INAO revision process exists, but proposal/opposition material is not treated as effective law by this tranche.

Executable rules promoted here:

- white standard: minimum natural alcohol 11.0%; yield 57 hL/ha; unconditional fermentable-sugar ceiling 3 g/L;
- red standard: minimum natural alcohol 10.5%; yield 50 hL/ha; sugar ceiling 2 g/L; malic acid ceiling 0.4 g/L;
- white Premier Cru: 11.5%; 55 hL/ha; 3 g/L unconditional sugar ceiling;
- red Premier Cru: 11.0%; 48 hL/ha; 2 g/L sugar ceiling; 0.4 g/L malic acid;
- market release is from 30 June of the year after harvest; the current engine represents the year offset but not exact day-level release semantics.

The official 11-climat color matrix is encoded explicitly in the site-claim layer rather than widening every climat to both colors.

## Puligny-Montrachet color matrix

The effective 2011 Puligny-Montrachet specification identifies three white-only Premier Cru climats: Hameau de Blagny, La Garenne, and Sous le Puits. The new site-specific claim constraints allow the other 14 seeded Premier Cru climats to be used on the red path without authorizing those three impossible combinations.

## Mercurey site supplement

`Clos du Château de Montaigu` is added through `named_sites_supplement.json` using the current 2025 Ministry Mercurey specification as direct provenance. The historical seed is left intact.
